# Liber

**The modern alternative to Calibre.** Read, convert and send your books, comics and manga to your eReader, in an app that doesn't look like 2008.

→ **[tryliber.com](https://tryliber.com)**

Liber is a desktop ebook library manager for **Windows, macOS and Linux**. It's free, and it has no ads and no account.

- **Your library, in order.** Drop in EPUB, PDF, AZW3, MOBI, CBZ or CBR files and they land in your library with covers and metadata. Already on Calibre? Liber reads your Calibre library as it is.
- **Send to your eReader in one click.** Pick your Kindle, Kobo, PocketBook or other eReader once. Liber converts each book to the right format and copies it over USB, one book or a whole shelf.
- **A reader built in.** EPUB, PDF, comics and manga, with themes, highlights and notes.
- **Find books.** Search Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, the Internet Archive and your own OPDS catalogues from inside the app.
- **Local-first.** Your books live in plain folders on your own disk. Liber indexes them and never uploads them. Encrypted cloud backup exists, it's optional, and it's off unless you turn it on.

## Status

Liber is in **early access** and hasn't been released yet. Join the list at [tryliber.com](https://tryliber.com) to get one email when it's ready.

When it ships, the installers will be published on this repository's **[Releases](../../releases)** page, which is also where the app's built-in updater gets new versions. Every update is signed, and the app checks the signature before installing it.

## About conversion

Converting between formats uses `ebook-convert`, from [Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com) (GPL v3, © Kovid Goyal). It isn't included in Liber. The first time you convert a book, Liber offers to download it separately, and you can remove it whenever you like from Settings.

Without it, everything else in Liber works as usual, and books that are already in the right format still copy over to your eReader.
